      How the level is worked out

      For each district the site looks at the wettest single day in the next three days, then adjusts for soaked ground and rising rivers.

      Wettest day aheadLevel
      Under 50 mmNormal
      50 to 75 mmWatch
      75 to 100 mmWatch, or Warning in a landslide-prone district
      100 to 150 mmWarning
      150 mm or moreSevere

      One level is added if more than 150 mm already fell in the past three days and at least 50 mm more is on the way. If the river flow near the district centre is forecast to reach 1.5 times today's level within three days, the district goes to at least Watch; at 2.5 times it goes to at least Warning.

      The 14 landslide-prone districts are the ones listed by the National Building Research Organisation: Badulla, Colombo, Galle, Gampaha, Hambantota, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Matale, Matara, Monaragala, Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura.

      Each district uses a single forecast point near its centre, so heavy rain in one corner can be missed. River flow comes from the GloFAS model and may describe a small stream rather than the main river.

      This is not an official warning service. For warnings, follow the Disaster Management Centre (hotline 117), the Department of Meteorology and NBRO.

      Data: Open-Meteo forecast, archive and flood APIs (GloFAS), USGS, GDACS. District outlines from GADM via thejeshgn/srilanka; Sinhala and Tamil names from SriLankaCitiesDatabase.
